Process

First, I understand where you are → then I build the clearest path to where you’re going.

Already have a design in Figma? Skip straight to step 4.

  1. Step01 01

    Intro call and a clear proposal

    • Your business and audience
    • Site goals
    • Checking the fit
    • A proposal with scope and price

    A 20-minute call. You tell me about the business, the audience, what the site needs to do and which pages you need, and I ask the questions that help me see the full picture. If it’s a good fit, you get a clear proposal with the scope, price and timeline.

    A clear proposalAt the end

  2. Step02 02

    Page structure within 48 hours

    • Order and message
    • Wireframe in Figma
    • A round of comments
    • Your approval before moving on

    Within 48 hours of the deposit, you get the page structure (a wireframe): what appears, in what order, and the message of each part. No colors or design yet, so we settle the story first and only then the look. On a multi-page site, we start with the home page. If the copy isn’t ready yet, I work with placeholder text.

    An approved structureAt the end

  3. Step03 03

    Full design for your approval

    • Design in Figma
    • Revision rounds
    • Every page
    • Approval before the build

    Based on the approved structure, I design the page in Figma: colors, typography, images and all the small details. This is where the revision rounds happen. On a multi-page site, every page goes through the same path: structure, then design. We move on to the build only once you’ve approved the whole design.

    An approved designAt the end

  4. Step04 04

    The build, with a live progress link

    • A one-to-one build
    • Animations
    • Responsive
    • SEO
    • CMS where needed

    The approved design becomes a real website, one to one: animations, responsive layouts, SEO, and a CMS for content that changes, if needed. As soon as the first page is built, you get a live link, so you can follow along and comment as it goes. I fix bugs and deviations from the approved design without limit.

    A launch-ready siteAt the end

  5. Step05 05

    Launch and training until the site is live

    • Domain setup
    • Final checks
    • Going live
    • Team training
    • Support after launch

    We connect the domain, run final checks on desktop, tablet and mobile, and the site goes live. Then a short training session for your team, so you can update the site yourselves. And after launch, I’m available for bug fixes and questions throughout the support period.

    A live websiteAt the end

FAQ

Which tools do you work with?

I design in Figma and build in Framer, Webflow or code with Claude Code. The tool depends on the project and on who will maintain the site afterwards, and we’ll choose what’s right for you together on the call.

Why not a $150 landing page?

A $150 page is usually a ready-made template with your content dropped in. With me, every page starts from a goal and an audience, is designed from scratch in Figma and is built around what you want visitors to do. It’s a different product, not a pricier version of the same thing.

What if we don’t like the design?

That’s why we work in stages. First we approve the page structure, then the design, with set revision rounds, and only then do we move on to the build.

How many revision rounds are there?

The rounds are on the design: a landing page gets 2 rounds, and a full site gets 2 rounds for the home page and 1 for the other pages. Before that there’s a round of comments on the page structure, which doesn’t count. During the build, I fix bugs and deviations from the approved design without limit. A build from an existing Figma design gets 1 revision round. Changes beyond that are priced separately.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit at the start, and the balance before the site goes live.

Who writes the copy?

The copy is your responsibility. If it isn’t ready yet, I start with placeholder text and swap in the real copy when it arrives.

Can we update the site ourselves after launch?

Yes. In Framer and Webflow your team can easily update text, images, posts and job listings, and I give you a training session at the end of the project. For a site built in code, we’ll agree up front on how it gets updated. And if you’d like, there’s also a monthly maintenance plan.

What happens on the intro call?

20 minutes. You tell me about the product and what the site needs to do, we see if it’s a good fit, and you leave with a few notes on your current site.
